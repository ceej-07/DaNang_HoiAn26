    Hội An custom clothes and shoes

    Start the order on Sunday and reserve Tuesday for a second fitting and pickup. Turnaround depends on the design and workshop workload, so confirm the deadline before paying.

    Custom clothing to compare

    • Kimmy Tailor — 70 Trần Hưng Đạo; an established tailor for made-to-measure garments. Website
    • BéBé Tailor — 05–07 Hoàng Diệu; broad fabric and garment choices. Website
    • 45 Thu Tailor — 06 Hoàng Diệu; made-to-measure clothing, convenient to the Old Town.
    • Blue Eye Tailor — 12 Lê Lợi; suits, shirts, trousers and dresses.

    Made-to-order shoes

    • Rô Shoe Shop — 26 Nguyễn Huệ; custom leather shoes, loafers, flats, boots and sandals.
    • Shoes Shop Hao — 26 Lê Lợi; made-to-order shoes and sandals.
    • Đông Châu Leather Shop — 24 Lê Lợi; custom leather footwear and leather goods.
    • Quỳnh Như Shoes Shop — 01 Trần Phú; family-run leather shoe and sandal workshop.

    Before you place an order

    • Bring 2–3 clear reference photos, including the back and side view; specify fabric, lining, buttons, pockets and length.
    • Ask for the full price, fabric type, number of fittings, delivery date and alteration policy in writing.
    • For shoes, have both feet measured, wear the socks you plan to use, and walk around the shop in the finished pair.
    • Do not leave Hội An until you have tried everything on. Keep Tuesday afternoon as a buffer for corrections.
    • Avoid ordering too many complicated pieces on a short trip; one well-fitted item is better than several rushed ones.

    Souvenir list

    Shops: Han Market and Con Market in Da Nang; Hội An Old Town for lanterns, silk, tailors and made-to-order leather shoes. Use the custom-shopping section to plan fittings.
